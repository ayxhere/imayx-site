Features

Everything a gateway needs.
Nothing a server costs.

The same options you'd expect from a server-side WhatsApp tool, packed into one native app with a clean AMOLED dashboard.

Multi-session

Link several WhatsApp numbers and manage each one separately from a single dashboard.

Smart auto-reply

Reply by keyword rules, or connect an AI provider of your choice for natural answers.

Scheduling

Queue messages for later and let the app deliver them on time, even with the screen off.

Webhooks & REST API

Forward incoming messages to your own URL and send through a local API from your apps.

Always-online & auto-read

Keep presence steady and mark chats as read automatically, with fine controls per session.

Anti-delete

Keep a local copy of messages that get deleted for everyone, stored only on your device.

Reactions

React to messages automatically or on demand, in single chats and groups.

Contacts & groups

Browse contacts, see group sender names, and control online and last-seen presence.

Built different

No server. Really.

Node.js and Baileys run inside the APK. The UI talks to a service on 127.0.0.1, so nothing is exposed to the internet unless you choose to.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is this an official WhatsApp app?

No. WhatsAyX is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta. "WhatsApp" is a trademark of its owner.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Session credentials and message data stay in the app's private storage. There is no WhatsAyX cloud. See the Privacy Policy.

Can my number get banned?

Automation with unofficial clients can break WhatsApp's terms and may lead to restrictions. Use it responsibly, avoid spam and bulk unsolicited messaging, and prefer a number you can afford to risk.

Does it work in the background?

Yes, through an Android foreground service with a notification. Some phone brands kill background apps, so you may need to disable battery optimization for WhatsAyX.

What does AI auto-reply send out?

Only if you turn it on and add your own provider key: the incoming message text is sent to that provider to generate the reply. Keyword replies never leave your phone.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 and above, on 64-bit (arm64) devices.
