1. Who we are

WhatsAyX ("the app", "we") is an Android app developed by imayx, an independent developer. The app runs a WhatsApp automation gateway locally on your own device.

We do not operate a backend for the app. Because of that, we are not able to see your messages, contacts, or credentials.

2. Data processed on your device

To work, the app handles the following data locally. It is stored in the app's private storage and is not sent to us.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number or location.
  • We do not receive your messages, contacts, media or session credentials.
  • We do not use advertising IDs, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data, because we do not have it.

4. Third parties and data you choose to send

The app connects to outside services only for features you use:

  • WhatsApp servers. To send and receive messages, the app talks to WhatsApp's servers using the same protocol as a linked device. WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy apply to that traffic.
  • AI providers (optional). If you enable AI auto-reply and add your own API key, the text of the incoming message is sent to the provider you picked to generate a reply. That provider's policy applies. Keyword auto-reply does not send anything out.
  • Webhooks (optional). If you add a webhook URL, incoming message events are sent to that URL. You are responsible for the server that receives them.
  • Updates and downloads. If a build offers an in-app link to download files, your device contacts that host directly.
If you turn on a feature that sends data to a third party, that data leaves your device at your request and is governed by that third party.

The app does not request access to your SMS, call logs, camera, microphone or location.

6. Storage and security

  • The gateway listens only on 127.0.0.1 (your device), so other devices on your network cannot reach it.
  • Credentials are kept in private app storage that is not included in Android backups.
  • Secrets are redacted from the in-app logs.

No system is perfectly secure. Keep your phone locked, avoid installing the app from untrusted sources, and do not share exported data.

7. Your control

  • Log out a session in the app to remove its credentials.
  • Clear data in Android settings, or uninstall the app, to delete everything the app stored.
  • Unlink the device from WhatsApp → Linked devices to revoke access at the source.

Since we hold no copy of your data, we cannot export or delete it on your behalf. You can do both directly on your device.
